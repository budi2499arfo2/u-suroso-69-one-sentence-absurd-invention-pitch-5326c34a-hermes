One sentence pitching a ridiculous invention: a sock that files a formal
complaint. The pitch is written as a complaint-desk log line — complaint
#4471, filed by 1 sock lost in the dryer at 09:14, claim auto-filed at
09:15, laundry fined $2, missing pair located in 4 minutes, status:
settled.

`main.py` writes the pitch to `/output/entry.txt` (one sentence, 157
characters), reads it back, and verifies it byte-for-byte.
